Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page describes how Hermes (a personal AI assistant application) handles the Google account data it accesses on behalf of the owner of the Google account that authorizes it.

What data is accessed

Hermes requests access to the following parts of your Google account via the Google OAuth 2.0 consent screen:

  • Read, send, and modify your Gmail messages
  • Read, create, and modify your Google Calendar events
  • Read, create, upload, and delete files in your Google Drive
  • Read your Google Contacts
  • Read, create, and modify Google Docs and Google Sheets

How the data is used

Hermes uses the accessed data only to perform the assistant tasks you request — for example, checking your calendar, drafting or sending email, searching or managing your Drive files, and reading or editing your documents. Hermes does not sell your data, and does not share it with any third party other than Google (the provider of the services being accessed).

Where the data is stored

Access credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored locally on the owner's own device, only in the files required for the application to function. No content of your emails, calendar, or files is stored in any server, log, or third-party service by Hermes.

Retention and deletion

You can delete all access at any time by revoking the application under your Google account (Google Account → Security → Third-party access and activity → Manage access, then remove this app) or by deleting the local token file on the device where Hermes runs. Once access is revoked, Hermes can no longer read or modify anything in your Google account.
